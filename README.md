## Why Is Certificate Attestation Required for Documents Used in the UAE?

Certificate attestation helps establish the authenticity of official signatures, seals and endorsements on documents intended for use in another country.

For example, a UAE employer may request an authenticated degree certificate as part of an employment or professional qualification process. A family relocating to the UAE may need authenticated marriage or birth certificates for relevant administrative procedures.

The UAE authorities require documents issued outside the country to undergo the applicable authentication before they can be accepted for official use.

The exact requirement depends on the document category, issuing authority and purpose of submission. Applicants should also confirm whether the employer, university or government department has any additional instructions.

## Understanding the UAE Attestation Process for Indian Documents

For Indian documents intended for use in the UAE, authentication generally involves Indian authorities followed by the UAE authentication stage.

The applicable route can vary according to the document category and current official procedures. The broad stages for eligible documents may include:

**Step 1: Preliminary document authentication**

The certificate may first require verification or authentication by the relevant state authority or designated department. Educational and personal documents can have different preliminary requirements.

**Step 2: Ministry of External Affairs attestation**

After the applicable preliminary authentication, the document proceeds through the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) process in India.

**Step 3: UAE Embassy attestation**

The document then undergoes the relevant UAE Embassy authentication process in India. The UAE Embassy in New Delhi currently provides a digital attestation service for eligible India-issued documents.

**Step 4: UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation**

The UAE digital attestation service combines UAE Embassy attestation in New Delhi and UAE MoFA attestation in the UAE in one application for eligible documents.

Applicants should check the current official requirements before submission because the applicable route may differ for particular document categories.

## What Is the UAE Digital Attestation Service?

The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs offers a Digital Attestation Service through selected UAE missions, including the Embassy in New Delhi.

For eligible Indian documents, the service combines two authentication stages:

- UAE Embassy attestation in New Delhi.
- UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation in the UAE.

Applicants initiate the application through the UAE MoFA website using UAE Pass, select the country of issuance and complete the required application and payment steps.

According to the official UAE Embassy guidance, the original physical document is required for the digital attestation process. A MoFA-approved service provider coordinates document collection, and the digitally attested document is subsequently delivered electronically, with the original returned through courier.

This is an important development for applicants because it changes how the final UAE authentication stages are coordinated. Applicants should check the official portal for current eligibility and submission instructions before proceeding.

## Five Checks Before Submitting Documents for UAE Attestation

### 1. Confirm the Purpose of Each Certificate

A document required for employment may have different submission requirements from one needed for a family visa or university admission.

Confirm the purpose with the receiving organisation before starting the process.

### 2. Check the Issuing Authority

The authority that issued a certificate determines its preliminary authentication requirements.

A degree certificate, birth certificate and commercial document should not automatically be treated as following the same route.

### 3. Verify Names and Other Personal Details

Check that the applicant's name, dates and other important details are consistent across the relevant documents.

If a discrepancy exists, clarify whether supporting documentation or correction is required before submission.

### 4. Confirm Original Document Requirements

Some document categories require original certificates. Avoid submitting documents without understanding the official requirements and obtaining an acknowledgement when originals are collected.

Also check whether the document is laminated. UAE MoFA's general attestation requirements state that documents must not be laminated.

### 5. Confirm the Latest UAE Submission Instructions

The UAE Embassy in New Delhi publishes document-specific guidance, including requirements for personal and commercial documents.

Applicants should consult the official instructions rather than relying entirely on outdated process descriptions or general online advice.

## Common Mistakes That Can Complicate UAE Certificate Attestation

Applicants can avoid several common issues by checking their documents before submission:

- Starting the process without confirming the UAE receiving organisation's requirements.
- Assuming every educational and personal certificate follows the same route.
- Confusing apostille with the specific UAE Embassy and MoFA attestation requirements.
- Submitting incomplete or inconsistent documents.
- Ignoring original-document requirements.
- Overlooking separate procedures for commercial invoices and certificates of origin.
- Relying on outdated information about embassy submission arrangements.

A document-specific assessment before submission can help reduce avoidable complications.
